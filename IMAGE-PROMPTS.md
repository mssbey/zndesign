# Image generation provenance

All nine assets were created with the built-in image_gen tool and saved locally in public/images. WebP optimization uses Sharp; no third-party brand photographs are used. These are illustrative demo assets, not verified ZN Design inventory or store photography.

Prompts used (shared constraints: landscape 3:2, warm muted cream/beige palette, natural daylight, realistic tactile fabric, no text, logos, watermark or people):

- hero.webp: Premium editorial bedroom photograph. Cream linen upholstered double storage bed, tall softly rounded rectangular headboard, ivory duvet and pillows, taupe throw, oak bedside tables, ceramic vase and branch, plaster wall molding, oak floor, wool rug. Three-quarter bed-level view.
- modern.webp: Low modern bed with wide vertically channeled beige upholstered headboard and matching storage divan base, cream bedding and chocolate throw, dark oak nightstand, limestone plaster wall. Entire bed in three-quarter view.
- mattress.webp: Thick white quilted mattress with beige fabric sides on low oak platform, no duvet, fully visible mattress, warm cream bedroom, side three-quarter view.
- kids.webp: Small single child's bed, rounded cream upholstered headboard and base, sage blanket and ivory duvet, oak stool, wooden toy, beige child's bedroom.
- fabric.webp: Extreme close-up of layered upholstery swatches: cream boucle, oatmeal linen, taupe woven textile and charcoal velvet on oak tabletop.
- headboard.webp: Close view of wide tall cream boucle upholstered headboard, elegant rounded upper corners, pillows at bottom, warm plaster wall.
- bedding.webp: Folded ivory linen duvet and two cream pillows on the foot of a bed, beige woven throw, close-up textile product photograph.
- base.webp: Beige fabric upholstered double storage divan base without headboard or mattress. Closed flat fabric top, two joined boxes, short oak feet, off-white studio backdrop.
- luna-detail.webp: Reference edit of hero.webp. Closer slightly side-on view of the exact same cream upholstered headboard, pillows and upper base. Preserve bed design, fabric color, linen texture, warm side sunlight and bedroom.

The original tool outputs remain under the Codex generated_images directory. Final project assets are self-contained under public/images.
